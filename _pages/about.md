**Transferable capabilities:** statistical analysis, data wrangling, visualisation, reproducible workflows and reporting.
{: .notice--info}

I'm a biomedical data scientist with a PhD in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, specialising in the analysis and interpretation of complex biological data.

My work sits at the intersection of bioinformatics, computational biology and data science. I use statistical and computational approaches to investigate biological questions, with particular expertise in single-cell genomics, transcriptomics and immune repertoire analysis.

Currently a postdoctoral researcher at QIMR Berghofer, I develop reproducible analytical workflows using R/Bioconductor, Quarto and Linux-based computing. My previous experience spans molecular biology research, experimental data analysis and clinical trial coordination, giving me an understanding of the full research process, from experimental design and data generation to analysis and interpretation.

I'm interested in applying my computational and analytical skills to challenges in genomics, healthcare, biotechnology and data-driven research, while continuing to learn new technologies and approaches.

What I Do
------
- **Transcriptomics and single-cell genomics:** Analysis of bulk and single-cell RNA-seq data, including quality control, cell-type annotation, differential expression, pathway enrichment and trajectory inference.
- **Immune repertoire analysis:** Integration of single-cell transcriptomics with paired TCR sequencing to investigate clonotype diversity, expansion and immune-cell states.
- **Statistical analysis and biological interpretation:** Replicate-aware differential expression, differential abundance, exploratory analysis and interpretation of complex biological datasets.
- **Reproducible computational workflows:** Development of structured, documented analysis workflows using R/Bioconductor, Quarto, Git/GitHub and Linux-based high-performance computing.

Featured Project
------
### [Single-cell RNA-seq and TCR Repertoire Analysis](/bioinformatics/tcr-scrnaseq-showcase/)

An end-to-end computational analysis exploring CD4⁺ T-cell responses to TNFR2 agonism, integrating single-cell transcriptomics with T-cell receptor sequencing.

The project demonstrates my approach to quality control, dimensionality reduction, cell-state identification, differential expression, pathway analysis and immune repertoire characterisation.

The analysis is organised as a reproducible Quarto book, combining documented code, visualisations and biological interpretation.

**[Explore the analysis →](https://serenagall.github.io/tcr-scrnaseq-showcase/)**
